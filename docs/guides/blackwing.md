# Blackwing (pure): play notes (expert guide)

Source: notes supplied with Step 4 (2026-09-30). `agents/profiles.py` (`BLACKWING`) is the pilot's
translation of them. The list is `edison/decks/blackwing.ydk`: 2nd of 385 at LDS Las Vegas Edison
(2026-05-09), 12-1-0 - the most match wins of the 511 legal pure Blackwing lists in the TopDeck data.

1. **Black Whirlwind is the engine.** While it is on the field, Normal Summon the least important
   Blackwing first (it may get removed) and search the card you need most. Whirlwind on the field is a
   big progress bonus.
2. **Bora the Spear:** often hold it in hand for a Whirlwind search rather than Special Summoning it
   immediately - unless the extra attacker or Synchro material wins the turn.
3. **Kalut the Moon Shadow** is worth more in hand (+1400 ATK in the damage step) than on the field.
   Hold it for a battle that matters, or for lethal.
4. **Shura the Blue Flame:** when it destroys a monster by battle it Special Summons a Blackwing with
   1500 or less ATK from the Deck (a Tuner enables a Main Phase 2 Synchro). Value setups that let Shura
   win battles (Gale's halving, Kalut).
5. **Gale the Whirlwind:** halve the biggest opposing monster; it is also a Tuner.
6. **Sirocco the Dawn:** one huge attack beats several blocked ones against Gorz / Tragoedia or a lone
   defender.
7. **Icarus Attack:** turn spare monsters into removal; best in response to removal targeting a
   Winged Beast.
